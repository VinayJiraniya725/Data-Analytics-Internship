# Project 4 – E-Commerce Sales Dashboard 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

This is **Project 4** of my Data Analytics Internship.

In this project, I created an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard using Power BI** to analyze sales performance and generate meaningful business insights from e-commerce data.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, orders, quantity, average order value, product performance, payment methods, monthly trends, yearly sales, referral sources, and sales target achievement.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and build an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps in:

- Tracking overall sales performance
- Analyzing product-wise sales
- Understanding monthly sales trends
- Comparing yearly sales performance
- Analyzing payment methods
- Evaluating referral sources
- Tracking sales target achievement
- Monitoring important business KPIs

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Sales by Product

Analyzes sales performance for different products, including:

- Chair
- Printer
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Monitor
- Desk
- Phone

### 2. Sales by Payment Method

Shows sales distribution across different payment methods:

- Credit Card
- Online
- Cash
- Gift Card
- Debit Card

### 3. Monthly Sales Trend

Shows monthly sales trends across different years and helps analyze changes in sales throughout the year.

### 4. Year-wise Sales

Compares sales performance across:

- 2023
- 2024
- 2025

### 5. Sales by Referral Source

Analyzes sales generated through:

- Instagram
- Email
- Google
- Facebook
- Referral

### 6. Sales Target Achievement

A gauge chart is used to monitor total sales against the defined sales target.
